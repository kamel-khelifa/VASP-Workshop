Foundations of DFT Simulations with VASP

A Two-Day Hands-On Workshop for Beginners

By Dr. Ahmed Redha Benrekia

About the Workshop

VASP (Vienna Ab initio Simulation Package) is one of the most powerful and widely used software packages for quantum mechanical calculations in materials science and chemistry. This workshop aims to demystify its "black box" and provide practical, hands-on experience for beginners.

Date 22-23 June 2026
Venue Conference Room, Research Laboratories Building, University of Medea
Location Google Maps Link

Workshop Goals

  • Understand core concepts and demystify the "black box" of VASP.
  • Learn basic principles of DFT and total energy calculations.
  • Apply VASP to real 3D systems with practical examples.
  • Discuss and overcome common input/output barriers.
  • Gain the confidence to use VASP independently for research.
